# **C5ISR, TAK, UAV & Cybersecurity Master Glossary**

## **Reference Architecture & Operational Terminology**

This reference manual standardizes terminology, operational definitions, and technical contexts across military command systems, uncrewed aerial systems (UAS), geospatial tactical awareness networks, and cyber defense architecture.

| Abbreviation (EN / PL) | Full Name (EN) | Full Name (PL) | Operational Definition & Technical Context | 
| ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- | 
| **25. BKPow** | 25th Air Cavalry Brigade | 25\. Brygada Kawalerii Powietrznej | Polish airmobile brigade operating tactical aviation and airborne forces, relevant to low-altitude airspace operations and UAV integration. | 
| **ADE** | Electronic Delivery Address | Adres do e-Doręczeń | Official digital mailbox address used in Poland for verified, legally binding electronic communication with state institutions. | 
| **ADS-B** | Automatic Dependent Surveillance–Broadcast | Automatyczne Zależne Dozorowanie – Rozgłaszanie | Aviation surveillance technology in which an aircraft determines its position via satellite navigation and periodically broadcasts it, enabling real-time airspace tracking alongside U-Space/UTM feeds. | 
| **AES** | Advanced Encryption Standard | Zaawansowany Standard Szyfrowania | Symmetric block cipher (128, 192, 256 bits) used to secure tactical radio links, CoT data streams, and encrypted data-at-rest across nodes. | 
| **AGL** | Above Ground Level | Nad Poziomem Terenu | Flight altitude measured relative to the ground elevation directly beneath the aircraft, essential for terrain clearance and tactical flight profiles. | 
| **A-ISR** | Acoustic Intelligence, Surveillance, and Reconnaissance | Akustyczny Wywiad, Obserwacja i Rozpoznanie | Passive sensor networks utilizing microphone arrays and acoustic signature analysis to detect, classify, and triangulate low-flying threats (e.g., UAVs, cruise missiles) without emitting RF signals. | 
| **AIS** | Automatic Identification System | System Automatycznej Identyfikacji | Tracking system used on ships and vessel traffic services for identifying and locating vessels by electronically exchanging data with other nearby ships and AIS base stations. | 
| **AMT** | Armenia Time | Czas Armeński | Standard time zone (UTC+4) used in Armenia and the Caucasus operational theater, critical for monitoring regional operations and strategic airspace. | 
| **AOR** | Area of Responsibility | Obszar Odpowiedzialności | Pre-defined geographic area assigned to a commander to perform or support military operations. | 
| **API** | Application Programming Interface | Interfejs Programowania Aplikacji | Set of rules and protocols that allows TAK plugins, services, and external command systems to communicate and exchange data seamlessly. | 
| **APT** | Advanced Persistent Threat | Zaawansowane Długotrwałe Zagrożenie | Stealthy threat actor, typically a state-sponsored group, conducting continuous, targeted computer hacking and cyber reconnaissance operations over long periods. | 
| **AR** | Augmented Reality | Rzeczywistość Rozszerzona | Overlay of digital graphics (e.g., CoT markers, range rings) onto real-time video feeds (EUD or heads-up displays) to enhance operator situational awareness. | 
| **AST (Arabia)** | Arabia Standard Time | Saudyjski Czas Standardowy | Standard time zone (UTC+3) spanning the Middle East (including Saudi Arabia, Iraq, Kuwait, Yemen), utilized for regional coordination and tactical timelines. | 
| **AST (Atlantic)** | Atlantic Standard Time | Czas Standardowy Atlantycki | UTC-4 time zone used in tactical planning and coordination across eastern North America, parts of the Caribbean, and Atlantic operational theaters. | 
| **ATAK** | Android Tactical Assault Kit | Androidowy Taktyczny Zestaw Szturmowy | Geospatial situational awareness platform for Android devices providing tactical map views, team tracking, targeting data, and plugin integration. | 
| **ATC** | Air Traffic Control | Kontrola Ruchu Lotniczego | Service provided by ground-based controllers to direct aircraft safely on the ground and through controlled airspace. | 
| **BDA** | Battle Damage Assessment | Ocena Szkód Bitewnych | Timely and accurate evaluation of damage resulting from the application of military force against a designated target. | 
| **BLOX-TAK** | BLOX-TAK Ecosystem | Ekosystem BLOX-TAK | Custom Edge Computing architecture and agent-based system designed to integrate distributed early warning sensor nodes with the TAK ecosystem. | 
| **BMS** | Battlefield Management System | System Zarządzania Polem Walki | Integrated software and hardware framework providing tactical commanders with a real-time Common Operational Picture (COP), facilitating C2 and situational awareness down to the vehicle and dismounted soldier level. | 
| **BOS** | Battlefield Operating System | System Operacyjny Walki | Integrated tactical functions (e.g., maneuver, intelligence, fire support, air defense, sustainment, command) required to execute operations. | 
| **BVLOS / VLOS** | Beyond Visual Line of Sight / Visual Line of Sight | Poza Zasięgiem Wzroku / W Zasięgu Wzroku | Operational flight modes defining whether a remote pilot maintains direct unaided visual contact with the UAV (VLOS) or operates over extended distances relying on C2 links and U-Space (BVLOS). | 
| **C2** | Command and Control | Dowodzenie i Kontrola | The exercise of authority and direction by a designated commander over assigned and attached forces in the accomplishment of the mission. | 
| **C3I** | Command, Control, Communications, and Intelligence | Dowodzenie, Kontrola, Łączność i Wywiad | Core architecture providing command structures with communications networks and intelligence inputs required for execution of operations. | 
| **C4ISR** | Command, Control, Communications, Computers, Intelligence, Surveillance, Reconnaissance | Dowodzenie, Kontrola, Łączność, Komputery, Wywiad, Obserwacja i Rozpoznanie | Framework integrating warfighting assets, sensors, and decision support networks to enable battlefield awareness and decision superiority. | 
| **C5ISR** | Command, Control, Communications, Computers, Cyber, Intelligence, Surveillance, Reconnaissance | Dowodzenie, Kontrola, Łączność, Komputery, Cyberprzestrzeń, Wywiad, Obserwacja i Rozpoznanie | Evolution of C4ISR incorporating defensive and offensive cyber operations directly into command structures and operational nodes. | 
| **CAS** | Close Air Support | Bliskie Wsparcie Lotnicze | Air action by fixed- or rotary-wing aircraft against hostile targets in close proximity to friendly forces, requiring detailed integration with surface maneuver. | 
| **CBAC** | Capability-Based Access Control | Kontrola Dostępów Oparta na Uprawnieniach | Security access architecture granting rights based on proven node capabilities, system roles, and cryptographic assertions rather than identity alone. | 
| **CET / CEST** | Central European Time / Central European Summer Time | Czas Środkowoeuropejski / Środkowoeuropejski Czas Letni | Standard time zone (UTC+1) and daylight saving time zone (UTC+2) applicable across central European operational theaters and NATO headquarters. | 
| **CGI** | Common Gateway Interface | Wspólny Interfejs Bramki | Standard protocol for web servers to execute console-like programs directly on a server to generate dynamic web pages or interface with backend systems. | 
| **COP** | Common Operational Picture | Wspólny Obraz Sytuacji Taktycznej | Single display of relevant information (e.g., position reports, threat tracks, operational boundaries) shared by multiple commands to foster collaborative planning. | 
| **CoT** | Cursor on Target | Cursor on Target (Format Danych Taktycznych) | XML-based data schema defining essential "What, Where, When" operational information across C2, TAK, and ISR sensor nodes. | 
| **C-UAS** | Counter-Unmanned Aircraft Systems | Systemy Zwalczania Bezzałogowych Statków Powietrznych | Hardware, software, kinetic, and electronic warfare systems deployed to detect, track, identify, neutralize, or destroy hostile UAVs. | 
| **Cyber Legion** | Cyber Legion Program | Program Cyber Legion | Polish Ministry of National Defence initiative aimed at harnessing civilian cyber talent and integrating volunteer technical skills into defensive cyber operations. | 
| **Datalink** | Data Link Network | Łącze Transmisji Danych | Radio RF link optimized for real-time transmission of digital data (video telemetry, control signals, CoT messages) between tactical assets. | 
| **DDoS** | Distributed Denial of Service | Rozproszona Odmowa Usługi | Cyberattack attempting to disrupt normal traffic of a targeted server, service, or tactical network by overwhelming the infrastructure with a flood of traffic. | 
| **DKWOC** | Cyber Defense Forces Component Command | Dowództwo Komponentu Wojsk Obrony Cyberprzestrzeni | Polish specialized military command responsible for conducting full-spectrum operations within cyberspace, including defense, reconnaissance, and offensive actions. | 
| **DLAN** | Tactical Local Area Network | Taktyczna Sieć Lokalna | High-bandwidth field network enabling IP-based communication, telemetry exchange, and video streaming between localized mobile units. | 
| **DR** | Dead Reckoning | Nawigacja Zliczeniowa | Process of calculating current position by using a previously determined position and advancing that position based upon estimated speed, elapsed time, and course. | 
| **Dron Legion** | Drone Legion Program | Program Dron Legion | Polish military framework program integrating civilian UAV operators, standardized training, and dual-use drone systems into the territorial defense structures. | 
| **DTED** | Digital Terrain Elevation Data | Cyfrowe Dane Wzniesień Terenu | Standard matrix format of elevation values used in military geographic information systems for line-of-sight calculations and 3D terrain rendering. | 
| **Dual-Use** | Dual-Use Technology | Technologia Podwójnego Zastosowania | Systems, software, and hardware architectures designed for both civilian applications (e.g., crisis management, U-Space safety) and military defense operations. | 
| **Ed25519** | Ed25519 Public-Key Signature System | System Podpisu Ed25519 (Krzywe Eliptyczne) | Modern elliptic-curve cryptography scheme offering high-speed, highly secure digital signatures and encryption, often utilized in high-security OPSEC environments like Tails. | 
| **Edge Computing** | Edge Computing | Przetwarzanie Brzegowe | Distributed computing paradigm bringing data processing, AI inference, and sensor fusion directly to localized field nodes to minimize latency and bandwidth usage. | 
| **EET / EEST** | Eastern European Time / Eastern European Summer Time | Czas Wschodnioeuropejski / Wschodnioeuropejski Czas Letni | Standard time zone (UTC+2) and DST (UTC+3) used heavily in operational tracking across Ukraine, the Black Sea, and the eastern NATO flank. | 
| **EO / IR** | Electro-Optical / Infrared | Elektrooptyka / Podczerwień | Dual-sensor payload systems providing optical visual spectrum imagery along with thermal radiation imaging for day/night reconnaissance. | 
| **EUD** | End User Device | Urządzenie Końcowe Użytkownika | Tactical smart device (e.g., Samsung Tactical Edition running ATAK or WinTAK) operated by field personnel to render COP and issue commands. | 
| **EW** | Electronic Warfare | Walka Elektroniczna | Military action involving the use of electromagnetic and directed energy to control the electromagnetic spectrum or to attack the enemy. | 
| **Failsafe** | Failsafe Logic Protocol | Protokół Asekuracyjny | Automated emergency response triggered in unmanned systems during loss of link, low power, or system fault (e.g., Auto-RTH, loiter, landed disarm). | 
| **FFI** | Friend or Foe Identification | Identyfikacja Swój-Obcy | Electronic system that uses radio signals to identify aircraft, ships, or vehicles as friendly, neutral, or hostile (often synonymous with IFF). | 
| **FLIR** | Forward-Looking Infrared | Obrazowanie w Podczerwieni | Thermal imaging technology that detects infrared radiation to build real-time tactical video feeds regardless of ambient light. | 
| **FMS** | Flight Management System | System Zarządzania Lotem | Fundamental avionics system that automates a wide variety of in-flight tasks, reducing the workload on the flight crew or drone operator. | 
| **FMV** | Full Motion Video | Obraz Wideo w Czasie Rzeczywistym | Continuous high-frame-rate video feed streamed from airborne assets (UAV, ISR pods) directly to EUDs or tactical operations centers (TOC). | 
| **FOA** | Field of Action | Pole Działania | Designated spatial volume or operational domain in which tactical personnel or automated assets are authorized to engage targets or run missions. | 
| **FOV** | Field of View | Pole Widzenia | Angular extent of a given scene or spatial sector that is imaged or observed by an optical/sensor payload at a given instance. | 
| **FPV** | First Person View | Widok z Pierwszej Osoby | Real-time low-latency video streaming method where the pilot controls an unmanned vehicle as if seated inside, using video goggles or EUD. | 
| **GCS** | Ground Control Station | Naziemna Stacja Kontroli | Hardware and software suit (tablet, rugged PC, antenna array) used by operators to command, control, and receive telemetry from a UAV. | 
| **GeoJSON** | Geographic JSON | Format GeoJSON | Open standard format designed for representing simple geographical features, along with their non-spatial attributes, based on JSON. | 
| **GIS** | Geographic Information System | System Informacji Geograficznej | Spatial framework for gathering, managing, and analyzing geospatial layers, tactical elevation profiles, vectors, and map imagery. | 
| **GLONASS** | Global Navigation Satellite System | Globalny Satelitarny System Nawigacyjny (GLONASS) | Russian space-based satellite navigation system operating as an alternative or complementary constellation to GPS. | 
| **GNSS** | Global Navigation Satellite System | Globalny Nawigacyjny System Satelitarny | Generic term for satellite navigation systems providing autonomous geo-spatial positioning with global coverage (GPS, GLONASS, Galileo, BeiDou). | 
| **GPS** | Global Positioning System | Globalny System Pozycjonowania | US space-based radio-navigation system providing precise position, velocity, and time (PVT) data to military and civilian receivers worldwide. | 
| **HAHO** | High Altitude High Opening | Skok z Dużej Wysokości z Wczesnym Otwarciem | Parachute insertion technique where military personnel jump at high altitude and open parachutes shortly after jumping to glide long distances undetected. | 
| **HALO** | High Altitude Low Opening | Skok z Dużej Wysokości z Późnym Otwarciem | Parachute insertion technique where military personnel jump at high altitude, freefall to low altitude, and open parachutes close to the ground. | 
| **IAM** | Identity and Access Management | Zarządzanie Tożsamością i Dostępem | Framework of policies and technologies ensuring that the right enterprise and field nodes have appropriate access to tactical data streams. | 
| **ICMP** | Internet Control Message Protocol | Protokół Komunikatów Kontrolnych Internetu | Network layer protocol used by network devices to diagnose communication issues, such as ping and traceroute operations. | 
| **ICS** | Industrial Control Systems | Przemysłowe Systemy Sterowania | General term for control systems used in industrial production, including SCADA systems, PLCs, and distributed control systems. | 
| **IED** | Improvised Explosive Device | Improwizowane Urządzenie Wybuchowe | Custom or improvised bomb constructed and deployed in ways other than conventional military action, requiring dedicated counter-measures. | 
| **IFF** | Identification Friend or Foe | Identyfikacja Swój-Obcy | Secure transponder system using encrypted RF interrogation/response formats to identify friendly military craft and prevent fratricide. | 
| **IMU** | Inertial Measurement Unit | Bezwładnościowa Jednostka Pomiarowa | Sensor package (gyroscopes, accelerometers, magnetometers) providing orientation, velocity, and gravitational force data for navigation without GPS. | 
| **IoBT** | Internet of Battlefield Things | Internet Rzeczy na Polu Walki | Network of interconnected sensors, wearables, unmanned platforms, and smart munitions communicating via secure tactical IP topologies. | 
| **IP** | Internet Protocol | Protokół Internetowy | Network-layer routing protocol providing addressable packet delivery across tactical radios, LANs, and strategic networks. | 
| **IRST / IRDT** | Iran Standard Time / Iran Daylight Time | Czas Irański / Irański Czas Letni | Time zone (UTC+3:30 / UTC+4:30) critical for tracking operations, drone launches, and cyber activities originating from or targeting Iran. | 
| **ISR** | Intelligence, Surveillance, and Reconnaissance | Wywiad, Obserwacja i Rozpoznanie | Integrated activities acquiring and processing operational data to inform command decision-making and operational planning. | 
| **IST / IDT** | Israel Standard Time / Israel Daylight Time | Czas Izraelski / Izraelski Czas Letni | Standard time zone (UTC+2) and daylight saving time (UTC+3) used in Israel, Gaza, and Levant operational theaters. | 
| **ITA** | Information Technology Architecture | Architektura Technologii Informacyjnych | Structural framework defining software, hardware, networking, and data exchange topologies across tactical command infrastructures. | 
| **ITAK** | iOS Tactical Assault Kit | iOSowy Taktyczny Zestaw Szturmowy | Adaptation of the TAK situational awareness framework natively built for Apple iOS devices. | 
| **JTAC** | Joint Terminal Attack Controller | Wysunięty Kontroler Naprowadzania Lotnictwa | Certified service member directing from a forward position the action of combat aircraft engaged in Close Air Support and air attacks. | 
| **JWD** | Command Support Unit | Jednostka Wsparcia Dowodzenia | Military organizational unit responsible for the deployment, maintenance, and security of command posts and C2 IT/communication networks. | 
| **KLV** | Key-Length-Value | Kodowanie Klucz-Długość-Wartość | Data encoding standard (STANAG 4609) used to embed synchronized telemetry, sensor footprint, and GPS coordinates directly into FMV streams in TAK. | 
| **KML / KMZ** | Keyhole Markup Language / Compressed KML | Język znaczników Keyhole / Skompresowany KML | XML notation format used to express geographic annotation, vector overlays, and 3D shapes within GIS applications and TAK suites. | 
| **KRZ** | National Register of Debtors | Krajowy Rejestr Zadłużonych | Polish state IT system centralizing data on insolvency and restructuring proceedings. | 
| **LDR** | Low Data Rate | Niska Przepustowość Danych | Narrowband tactical transmission profile optimized for high noise, jammed, or non-line-of-sight environments where bandwidth is constrained. | 
| **LGB** | Laser-Guided Bomb | Bomba Kierowana Laserowo | Guided bomb that uses semi-active laser homing to strike a designated target with extreme precision. | 
| **LiDAR** | Light Detection and Ranging | Wykrywanie i Ranging Światłem (LiDAR) | Remote sensing method using pulsed laser light to measure distances and create high-resolution 3D point clouds of terrain and structures. | 
| **LM** | Loitering Munition | Amunicja Krążąca | Weapon system capable of loitering passively over an operational area to search for, track, and kinetically strike targets upon identification. | 
| **LOS** | Line of Sight | Linia Wzroku / Linia Widzenia | Direct unobstructed path between a transmitter and receiver or between an optical payload sensor and its intended ground target. | 
| **LPI / LPD** | Low Probability of Intercept / Low Probability of Detection | Niskie Prawdopodobieństwo Przechwycenia / Wykrycia | Tactical communications characteristics (spread spectrum, power control, directional antennas) designed to evade enemy SIGINT/EW detection. | 
| **LSS** | Low, Slow, and Small | Niskie, Wolne i Małe (Cele Powietrzne) | Threat classification describing small tactical UAVs and loitering munitions operating at low altitudes and speeds, making them difficult to detect by conventional radar. | 
| **LTE** | Long-Term Evolution | Standard Długoterminowej Ewolucji | Wireless broadband communication standard providing high-speed cellular networking, adaptable for commercial or private tactical deployments. | 
| **LUKS** | Linux Unified Key Setup | Standard Szyfrowania Dysków LUKS | Disk encryption specification for Linux platforms securing data-at-rest and highly sensitive tactical archives against unauthorized physical access. | 
| **MAC** | Media Access Control / Mandatory Access Control | Adres Sprzętowy / Obowiązkowa Kontrola Dostępów | Hardware address assigned to network interfaces, or a strict OS-level access model restricting resource usage based on security labels. | 
| **MALE** | Medium-Altitude Long-Endurance | Średnia Wysokość Długotrwałość Działań | Class of unmanned aerial vehicles operating between 10,000 to 30,000 feet for sustained durations (typically exceeding 24 hours). | 
| **MANET** | Mobile Ad-hoc Network | Mobilna Sieć Ad-hoc | Self-configuring, infrastructure-less network of mobile devices connected dynamically via wireless links, ideal for decentralized tactical units. | 
| **MDC2** | Multi-Domain Command and Control | Wielodomenowe Dowodzenie i Kontrola | Integrated C2 architecture synchronizing operational data, sensors, and effectors across air, land, maritime, space, and cyber domains. | 
| **MGRS** | Military Grid Reference System | Wojskowy System Odniesienia Siatki | Standard NATO geographic coordinate system representing positions on Earth using alphanumeric grid references down to 1-meter precision. | 
| **MIL-STD-2525 / APP-6** | Military Standard 2525 / NATO Joint Military Symbology | Standard Symboliki Wojskowej USA / NATO | Standardized graphical symbology frameworks used across TAK and C5ISR systems to represent friendly, neutral, unknown, and hostile tracks on a COP. | 
| **MIMO** | Multiple Input, Multiple Output | Wielowejściowy i Wielowyjściowy | Radiocommunications spatial diversity tech utilizing multiple transmit and receive antennas to increase throughput and link resilience. | 
| **MON** | Ministry of National Defence | Ministerstwo Obrony Narodowej | Supreme Polish government administration office in charge of the national defense apparatus and the Polish Armed Forces. | 
| **MSK** | Moscow Time | Czas Moskiewski | UTC+3 time zone used operationally across western Russia, parts of eastern Europe, and specific regional military sectors. | 
| **MSL** | Mean Sea Level | Średni Poziom Morza | Standard reference altitude base measured relative to global sea level, used for true aviation barometric elevation standardizations. | 
| **MVT** | Moving Vehicle Target | Ruchomy Cel Pojazdowy | Dynamic operational track designation assigned by tracking algorithms or ground teams to target assets traveling across road networks. | 
| **NIST** | National Institute of Standards and Technology | Narodowy Instytut Standardów i Technologii | US federal agency publishing cybersecurity frameworks, cryptographic standards (FIPS), and zero-trust guidelines. | 
| **NLOS** | Non-Line-of-Sight | Poza Linią Wzroku | Propagation scenario where radio signals travel between nodes without a direct visual or electromagnetic path, relying on reflection and diffraction. | 
| **NMEA** | National Marine Electronics Association Standard | Standard NMEA | Protocol standard for communication between marine electronics and GPS receivers, outputting ASCII sentences containing PVT data. | 
| **NTP** | Network Time Protocol | Protokół Czasu Sieciowego | Networking protocol for clock synchronization between computer systems over packet-switched, variable-latency data networks. | 
| **NVG** | Night Vision Goggles | Gogle Noktowizyjne | Optoelectronic device that allows images to be produced in levels of light approaching total darkness by amplifying ambient photons. | 
| **OCT** | Open Command & Telemetry | Otwarta Komenda i Telemetria | Non-proprietary control protocol schema used for standardizing telemetry streams and command inputs across diverse UAS hardware fleets. | 
| **OLiOC** | Civil Protection and Civil Defense | Ochrona Ludności i Obrona Cywilna | Polish national crisis management framework focused on protecting civilians, maintaining infrastructure, and coordinating emergency services (including SAR) during peacetime disasters and armed conflicts. | 
| **OLLOC** | OLLOC Early Warning System | System Wczesnego Ostrzegania OLLOC | Civilian dual-use early warning initiative deploying distributed acoustic-visual nodes to protect low-altitude airspace against aerial threats. | 
| **OPSEC** | Operational Security | Bezpieczeństwo Operacyjne | Analytical process and security discipline identifying critical information and eliminating digital or procedural footprints to prevent observation by adversaries. | 
| **OTAR** | Over-The-Air Rekeying | Zdalna Wymiana Kluczy Szyfrujących | Cryptographic operation re-keying remote tactical radios or security tokens wirelessly over an encrypted communication link. | 
| **P2P** | Peer-to-Peer | Architektura Równorzędna | Decentralized communications model where node pairs maintain equal capabilities and can initiate session streams directly without a central server. | 
| **PGP** | Pretty Good Privacy | Całkiem Dobra Prywatność (PGP) | Encryption program that provides cryptographic privacy and authentication for data communication, widely used for securing tactical emails and files via public-key cryptography. | 
| **PIM** | Protocol Independent Multicast | Multicast Niezależny od Protokołu | Family of multicast routing protocols for IP networks that provide one-to-many and many-to-many distribution of data over a LAN or WAN. | 
| **PKI** | Public Key Infrastructure | Infrastruktura Klucza Publicznego | System of digital certificates, certificate authorities, and public/private key pairs used to authenticate EUDs, servers, and users. | 
| **PLI** | Position Location Information | Informacja o Lokalizacji Pozycji | Continuous operational data stream delivering real-time geo-spatial coordinates and status updates of friendly personnel and assets. | 
| **PNT** | Positioning, Navigation, and Timing | Pozycjonowanie, Nawigacja i Synchronizacja Czasu | Core capability combining spatial location, directional orientation, and precise temporal synchronization necessary for military operations. | 
| **PoE** | Power over Ethernet | Zasilanie przez Ethernet | Technology allowing network cables to carry electrical power to devices such as tactical cameras, antennas, and wireless access points. | 
| **PTO** | Power Take-Off | Odbiór Mocy | Auxiliary mechanic/electrical output system powering external payloads, sensors, or specialized hardware on military platforms. | 
| **QoS** | Quality of Service | Jakość Usługi | Network management protocols prioritizing critical operational packets (e.g., voice, CoT streams) over lower-priority background traffic. | 
| **RAM** | Random Access Memory / Radar Absorbent Material | Pamięć o Dostępie Swobodnym / Materiał Pochłaniający Fale Radarowe | Volatile computing memory used by applications, OR specialized material coatings designed to reduce radar cross-section (RCS) of stealth aircraft. | 
| **RCS** | Radar Cross-Section | Skuteczna Powierzchnia Odbicia Radarowego | Measure of how detectable an object is by radar; a larger RCS indicates that an object is easier to detect. | 
| **Remote ID (RID)** | Remote Identification | Zdalna Identyfikacja (Dronów) | Digital "license plate" system required under U-Space regulations whereby UAVs broadcast identity, location, and ground station coordinates via RF/Network. | 
| **REST** | Representational State Transfer | REST (Architektura Usług Sieciowych) | Software architectural style that uses HTTP requests to access and manipulate data, widely used in web-based TAK extensions and map services. | 
| **RF** | Radio Frequency | Częstotliwość Radiowa | Electromagnetic wave frequencies ranging from 3 kHz to 300 GHz, utilized for wireless data links, radar, and electronic warfare. | 
| **RMF** | Risk Management Framework | Ramy Zarządzania Ryzykiem | Structured process for integrating cybersecurity and risk management activities into enterprise and battlefield system lifecycles. | 
| **RON** | Ministry of National Defence (Domain) | Resort Obrony Narodowej | Polish structural designation encompassing the entire Ministry of National Defence and the Armed Forces, commonly serving as the official military IT domain. |

| **RPO** | Commissioner for Human Rights (Ombudsman) | Rzecznik Praw Obywatelskich | Independent Polish constitutional authority investigating complaints concerning violations of law and human rights by state institutions. |

| **SALUTE** | Size, Activity, Location, Unit, Time, Equipment | Rozmiar, Działalność, Lokalizacja, Jednostka, Czas, Wyposażenie | Expanded tactical reporting format providing comprehensive reconnaissance details to higher headquarters. |

| **SEAD** | Suppression of Enemy Air Defenses | Tłumienie Obrony Powietrznej Wroga | Military action to neutralize, destroy, or temporarily degrade surface-based enemy air defenses by destructive or disruptive means. |
